Our story

Started small, in our own city

I started Shonen Style two years ago because I could never find clothes in Bhind that looked like me. Everything in the market looked the same, so I decided to build a shop where you decide the design.

My friends were the first customers. They brought anime art, college names and their own sketches, and I printed them one by one. Today we are still a small shop, and I still talk to every customer myself.
